# SimpleSync

**SimpleSync** is a Minecraft mod that automatically backs up and synchronizes your single-player worlds directly to your Google Drive account.

---

## Features

- **Automatic World Sync**: Seamlessly backs up and syncs your single-player worlds to Google Drive in the background when entering or leaving worlds.
- **Cross-Device Progress**: Play seamlessly on different computers while keeping your latest world progress up to date.
- **Conflict Detection**: Warns you if a world was modified on another device and lets you choose which version to keep.
- **Cloud World Manager**: View, download, restore, or delete your cloud saves right from an in-game menu.
- **Flexible Deletion**: Choose whether to delete both local and cloud copies or keep a backup in Google Drive when deleting a world.

---

## Getting Started

1. **Requirements**:
   - Minecraft 26.2
   - Fabric Loader
   - Fabric API

2. **Installation**:
   - Download the SimpleSync mod `.jar` file and place it inside your `.minecraft/mods` folder.

3. **Setup**:
   - Launch Minecraft and follow the easy on-screen prompt to connect your Google Drive account.

4. **Enjoy**:
   - SimpleSync handles backups and synchronization automatically in the background!
